Welcome to
the English
Game

An immersive challenge to practice and improve your english level through an inter-school competition. For one week, you will work with your team to design a full serious game. Be prepared to get involved, create the best game and challenge yourself!

Discover the program

What does the challenge have in store for you?

An experience based on fun, learning and practice.

We hope that your week will be filled with learning and hands-on experience.

The basics of serious games through videos and quizzes

A team of serious games experts will join you to share their experience and teach you the fundamentals of game mechanics.

Tools to design a project from A to Z

Each day will be based on a design process. From Monday to Friday, you will be supported as you design your project.
